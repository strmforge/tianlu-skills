# Supplier Diversity Certification Readiness Review

Status: incubating.

## Signal

External feeds surface a recurring document-heavy workflow around WOSB or EDWOSB, HUBZone, DBE or UCP, and state MWBE or MBE or WBE certifications. The useful solo-operator mechanism is not "get certified," "win set-aside contracts," or "unlock supplier-diversity revenue." It is a preparation workflow: turn official certification rules, application checklists, maps, forms, and owner-supplied business records into a source inventory, pathway classification, eligibility-precheck checklist, document ledger, reviewer queue, renewal tracker, and no-submit/no-account-action handoff.

In the current scan, read-only Chrome/X results were low volume but specific. `WOSB certification` and `HUBZone certification` surfaced recertification, webinar, and set-aside language. Generic `supplier diversity registration`, `MWBE certification`, and `DBE certification paperwork` were sparse or empty. That shape matters. It suggests a real operations-service lane, but not a hype-heavy feed category. GitHub results were also weak and scattered, which reinforces that this candidate should be framed as document and process readiness rather than as an open-source automation stack.

## Source

- SBA, Women-Owned Small Business Federal Contract program: WOSB and EDWOSB benefits apply to federal contracting opportunities and set-asides only; certification is required through MySBA Certifications; firms must be small under SBA size standards, at least 51% owned and controlled by women who are U.S. citizens, and women must manage day-to-day operations and long-term decisions; EDWOSB adds economic-disadvantage thresholds.
- SBA, HUBZone program: HUBZone certification supports federal set-asides and a price-evaluation preference; firms must be small, meet ownership and control rules, have a principal office in a HUBZone, and have at least 35% of employees living in a HUBZone; the HUBZone map is an official boundary surface; recertification is required every three years.
- MySBA Certifications: official no-cost SBA certification portal; provides should-I-apply guidance, preparation resources, HUBZone calculator access, and a one-stop portal across SBA certification programs.
- Caltrans, DBE Certification Information: official state UCP example referencing 49 C.F.R. Part 26, Subpart D; DBE applicants must meet citizenship, socially and economically disadvantaged ownership, personal net worth, average annual gross receipts, 51% ownership and control, independence, and management-control requirements; official application package, personal net worth statement, and certifying-agency roster are part of the preparation surface.
- New York State ESD, MWBE Certification Eligibility Requirements: MWBE certification requires 51% ownership, operation, and control by qualifying minority owners and or women; personal net worth restrictions, current personal and business federal and state tax records, employee-count limits, active-business requirements, and authority to do business in New York are explicit.
- Community and GitHub search samples: useful only as weak ecosystem and search-language evidence. They do not prove demand, approval likelihood, contract wins, compliance sufficiency, or repeatable solo-operator revenue.

## Proposed Trigger

Use this candidate when a founder, small business owner, government-contracting assistant, procurement consultant, supplier-diversity consultant, MWBE or DBE coach, APEX-style helper, or solo operator needs a WOSB or EDWOSB readiness packet, HUBZone precheck, DBE or UCP application-prep checklist, state MWBE evidence packet, supplier-diversity certification source map, ownership and control evidence ledger, personal-net-worth document checklist, employee-residency review packet, recertification tracker, or no-submit/no-contact/no-account-action handoff.

Use it especially when the task is preparation and evidence organization before official certification review. Do not use it as a certification decision, eligibility decision, net-worth determination, control determination, agency-contact workflow, application-submission workflow, supplier-portal workflow, or contract-win promise.

## Proposed Mechanism

0. Classify evidence strength before framing the opportunity.
   - L0: X post, webinar post, consultant landing page, set-aside anecdote, course post, certification-success post, or revenue claim. Use only as pain language and search language.
   - L1: public consultant pages, local-assistance pages, webinar pages, supplier-diversity overviews, or non-official workflow summaries. Treat as workflow-shape evidence, not certification or demand proof.
   - L2-lite: official SBA program pages, MySBA Certifications pages, official UCP or state-certification pages, official regulations, official application packages, official personal-net-worth forms, and official map or calculator surfaces. Treat as process and terminology authority, not applicant-specific approval authority.
   - L2-strong: owner-supplied organizational records, tax records, payroll records, leases, employee-address evidence, personal-net-worth statements, current application notices, certifier requests, or scoped engagement evidence.
1. Define the certification boundary.
   - Name program, certifying body, jurisdiction, business entity, NAICS or service category where relevant, target contract lane, source snapshot, allowed artifact, and excluded actions.
   - Separate preparation from account creation, portal action, application filing, upload, notarization, declaration, signature, certification claim, recertification filing, agency contact, and bid or contract action.
2. Inventory official and jurisdiction-specific sources.
   - Capture program pages, regulations, checklists, application packages, personal-net-worth forms, map or calculator surfaces, employee-count or receipts rules, ownership and control rules, recertification cadence, and support resources.
   - Record URL or file name, source date, jurisdiction, program, source role, stale-source risk, and whether the source is official, state-specific, owner-supplied, or L0/L1 signal.
3. Classify the pathway.
   - Separate WOSB or EDWOSB, HUBZone, DBE or UCP, state MWBE, ownership and control review, principal-office or employee-residency review, personal-net-worth and tax-document review, initial certification, and renewal or recertification paths.
   - Use statuses such as `source-supported candidate`, `needs ownership-control review`, `needs map check`, `needs employee-residency proof`, `needs tax and PNW worksheet`, `needs state-specific review`, `needs certifier review`, `conflict`, `stale`, and `ready for owner review`.
4. Build the business and document ledger.
   - For each program path, record entity, owner, citizenship or residency rule, ownership percentage, control role, principal-office state, employee-count rule, employee-residency requirement, receipts threshold, personal-net-worth rule, required tax years, required supporting documents, reviewer role, missing item, conflicting item, renewal date, and forbidden next action.
   - Keep tax, personal-net-worth, payroll, address, citizenship, and ownership documents as sensitive. Prefer pointers, redacted summaries, or document labels unless scoped retention is explicitly approved.
5. Prepare the reviewer queue.
   - Turn gaps into questions for the owner, CPA or tax preparer, bookkeeper, attorney, HR or payroll contact, office or lease contact, certifying-agency reviewer, or program-specific support resource.
   - Do not contact any certifying agency, SBA office, state office, UCP reviewer, landlord, employee, or third party without scoped approval.
6. Review account, form, and declaration boundaries.
   - Mark MySBA account creation, official portal login, application submission, supporting-document upload, personal-net-worth affidavit submission, declaration or notarization, status-check action, and agency email or phone contact as owner-approved follow-up only.
   - Treat MySBA Certifications, state certification portals, UCP systems, supplier-diversity application systems, and any public map or calculator that collects business data as account or action surfaces, not read-write permission.
7. Package the owner handoff.
   - Provide source inventory, pathway classification, eligibility-precheck checklist, business and document ledger, reviewer queue, renewal tracker, open questions, and no-submit/no-certify/no-account-write/no-contact/no-contract-win-claim receipt.
   - Route executable actions to operations handoff only after explicit scoped approval.

## Initial Scope

This candidate is for source mapping, evidence organization, conservative draft support, application-prep checklists, reviewer handoff, and owner decision support. It must not create or access official certification accounts, submit applications, upload supporting documents, sign or notarize forms, contact certifying agencies, decide ownership or control status, decide personal-net-worth qualification, decide HUBZone or DBE eligibility, claim certification, update vendor or supplier records, submit bids, promise set-aside access, promise supplier-diversity acceptance, promise contract wins, make legal, tax, accounting, HR, or compliance conclusions, or retain sensitive business-owner records without scoped approval.

It should produce a supplier-diversity certification source inventory, pathway classification, eligibility-precheck checklist, business and document ledger, reviewer queue, renewal tracker, open questions, risk boundary, and no-action receipt.

## Known Failure Modes

- Treating public certification pages as proof that a business qualifies or will be certified.
- Treating certification as the same as supplier onboarding, vendor approval, bid eligibility, award eligibility, or contract-win probability.
- Collapsing WOSB or EDWOSB, HUBZone, DBE or UCP, and state MWBE rules into one generic checklist.
- Applying one state's employee-count, net-worth, tax, or business-age requirement to another program or jurisdiction.
- Treating ownership percentage as enough without actual control and day-to-day management evidence.
- Missing principal-office location, employee-residency, receipts, or recertification boundaries.
- Logging into MySBA or state portals, uploading forms, signing, notarizing, declaring, or contacting agencies without scoped approval.
- Retaining excessive tax returns, payroll records, personal-net-worth statements, home-address details, citizenship records, or employee-location records.
- Treating X posts, consultant pages, webinar pages, or sparse GitHub projects as proof of paid demand, certification outcome, or repeatable revenue.
