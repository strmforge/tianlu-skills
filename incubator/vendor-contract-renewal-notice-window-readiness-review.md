# Vendor Contract Renewal And Notice-Window Readiness Review

Status: incubating.

## Signal

External feeds, operator complaints, and public contract-management pages now surface a recurring solo-operator workflow around vendor contracts that quietly auto-renew, notice windows buried in clauses, renewal reminders trapped across emails and PDFs, renewal calendars that nobody keeps current, and unwanted renewals discovered too late. The useful mechanism is not "send the notice," "cancel the contract," "approve the renewal," "negotiate the price," "sign the amendment," or "tell the owner the legal effect of the clause." It is a preparation workflow: turn contract sources, notice-window language, renewal dates, reminder surfaces, inbox or PDF evidence, and reviewer notes into a contract source inventory, clause and notice-window ledger, renewal calendar queue, alert queue, reviewer queue, and a no-action handoff.

In the current scan, direct legal-style X searches were mixed, but the useful signals stabilized once the phrasing moved to `auto renew`, `auto-renewal clause`, `notice window`, and `renewal calendar`. The same-day primary-source pass made the lane shape much clearer. Renewly packages contract inbox, notice-window extraction, auto-renewal-trap alerts, renewal alerts, calendar sync, and PDF extraction. Gatekeeper packages zero-missed-renewals, automated handoffs, workflow-driven approvals, renewal forecasting, and unwanted-renewal prevention. ContractSafe packages contract deadline reminders, auto-renew tracking, customizable alert dates, calendar views, and email reminders sent ahead of notification dates. SpotDraft surfaces `automatically renew` matches, expiration dates, and renewal analytics. That is strong enough to separate this lane from SaaS spend and license visibility. The method is still about source inventory, deadline extraction, and reviewer handoff, not about legal or commercial action.

## Proposed Trigger

Use this candidate when a founder, operations lead, procurement reviewer, finance or legal ops assistant, contract coordinator, or solo operator needs a vendor contract renewal readiness packet, notice-window review packet, auto-renewal-trap review packet, renewal calendar queue, or deadline-alert cleanup packet before any legal, commercial, or portal action.

Use it especially when the task is preparation, classification, and evidence organization before sending notices, cancelling or renewing contracts, negotiating amendments, signing documents, writing to a contract system, updating a calendar-of-record, making payments, or contacting vendors. Do not use it as legal authority, notice authority, cancellation authority, renewal authority, negotiation authority, payment authority, e-sign authority, portal authority, or system-write authority.

## Proposed Mechanism

0. Classify evidence strength before framing the opportunity.
   - L0: X complaints, operator threads, "we almost missed the deadline," "the clause was buried in a PDF," "all the contracts are in email," or "we do not know what auto-renews" chatter. Use only as friction language and search language.
   - L1: vendor blogs, consultant posts, CLM marketing, or contract-reminder service pages. Treat as workflow-shape evidence, not legal, commercial, or payment authority.
   - Public product pages, request-demo flows, and feature pages can prove that offer packaging exists around this pain. Record them as offer-existence and workflow-shape evidence only, not paid proof or action authority.
   - L2-lite: official or product-controlled pages where reminder mechanics, renewal repository mechanics, contract-source mechanics, or workflow boundaries are explicit enough to support lane design.
   - L2-strong: owner-supplied contracts, order forms, amendments, renewal reminders, inbox artifacts, PDF extracts, clause excerpts, deadline calculations, reviewer notes, and scoped no-action replay packets.
1. Split the workflow into lanes before doing anything else.
   - Lane A: contract source inventory covering agreement type, version, source location, counterparty, internal owner, and stale-or-missing-source risk.
   - Lane B: clause and notice-window extraction covering term length, auto-renewal posture, notice window, cancellation deadline, renewal date, recipient or method language if present, and ambiguity cues.
   - Lane C: renewal calendar and alert queue covering reminder dates, escalation points, missing reminders, duplicate reminders, inbox-only reminders, and manual review checkpoints.
   - Lane D: reviewer and decision-support lane covering owner questions, finance questions, procurement questions, legal-review flags, and contract-status handoff.
   - Neighbor lane only when needed: SaaS spend and license visibility. Treat app roster, seat waste, duplicate-tool visibility, and usage-plus-spend linkage as adjacent but separate unless the packet explicitly needs both.
   - Name source snapshot, allowed artifact, and excluded actions for each lane.
2. Inventory official sources and readback surfaces.
   - Capture contracts, order forms, amendments, renewal reminders, inbox evidence, PDF or repository sources, calendar notes, workflow notes, stale or broken source risks, and login-gated source risks.
   - Record URL or file name, source date, vendor, lane type, field or artifact type, and whether the source is official, owner-supplied, dashboard readback, inbox evidence, PDF extract, login-gated, or L0 or L1 signal.
3. Classify the evidence packet and its risks.
   - Separate source facts, clause facts, deadline facts, reminder facts, contract-owner facts, reviewer facts, stale source, missing source, ambiguity, renewal exposure, payment cue, negotiation cue, and legal-review cue.
   - Use statuses such as `source-supported candidate`, `needs contract readback`, `needs owner review`, `needs finance review`, `needs procurement review`, `needs legal review`, `stale`, `broken`, `login-gated`, `conflict`, and `ready for owner handoff`.
4. Build the contract renewal ledgers.
   - For each agreement, record vendor, product or service, agreement type, source pointer, term, auto-renewal posture, renewal date, notice window, cancellation deadline, reminder date or missing-reminder cue, amendment cue, owner, inbox evidence, PDF evidence, conflicting source, stale source, reviewer role, and forbidden next action.
   - Keep contract IDs, signer names, personal emails, billing details, payment details, vendor contacts, internal contact lists, clause text dumps, and exportable source data as sensitive. Prefer pointers, redacted summaries, or field labels unless scoped retention is explicitly approved.
5. Prepare the reviewer queue.
   - Turn gaps into questions for the owner, finance reviewer, procurement reviewer, legal reviewer, contract manager, or system owner.
   - Do not contact vendors, counterparties, employees, finance, legal, procurement, or internal teams without scoped approval.
6. Review action boundaries.
   - Mark notice submission, cancellation, renewal approval, negotiation, signature, payment, portal writes, calendar-of-record writes, repository writes, and workflow-state changes as owner-approved follow-up only.
   - Treat contract-management platforms, inboxes, shared drives, procurement tools, finance tools, and legal tools as account or action boundaries, not read-write permission.
7. Package the owner handoff.
   - Provide contract source inventory, clause and notice-window ledger, renewal calendar queue, alert queue, reviewer queue, open questions, and no-notice-send/no-cancel/no-renewal-approve/no-negotiate/no-payment/no-sign/no-portal-write/no-system-write receipt.
   - Route executable actions to operations handoff or draft-and-evidence support only after explicit scoped approval.

## Initial Scope

This candidate is for source mapping, clause and deadline readback interpretation, evidence organization, conservative draft support, reviewer handoff, and owner decision support. It must not interpret contract law, decide legal effect, send or file notices, cancel or renew contracts, negotiate commercial terms, sign amendments, approve payments, update calendars of record, update contract systems, or retain sensitive contract, billing, email, vendor-contact, or payment data without scoped approval.

It should produce a contract source inventory, clause and notice-window ledger, renewal calendar queue, alert queue, reviewer queue, open questions, risk boundary, and no-action receipt.

## Known Failure Modes

- Treating clause extraction, reminders, repositories, and approvals as one uniform workflow instead of separate lanes.
- Treating a product page, savings claim, or prevented-renewal claim as proof that customer payment, safe cancellation, or legal correctness has been demonstrated.
- Treating a clause summary as legal interpretation or cancellation authority.
- Treating SaaS seat or license visibility as the same workflow as contract notice-window tracking.
- Missing notice method, recipient, timezone, or version conflicts while pretending the deadline is settled.
- Treating inbox or PDF evidence as permission to write to a calendar, portal, repository, or system-of-record.
- Logging into contract or procurement tools to send notices, change statuses, sign, pay, or contact vendors without scoped approval.
- Retaining excessive contract, inbox, billing, payment, vendor, or internal-contact data.
